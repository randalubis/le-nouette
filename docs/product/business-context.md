## 2. Business context

### 2.1 Current business

**LOCKED**

- Le Nouette sells one cheese-stick flavor in two package formats.
- The product is a cheese-flavored flour stick covered with Edam and cheddar cheese.
- The physical stick is thin and ribbon-like. This shape helps retain crispness and crunch longer than a blockier stick.
- The business is run by two founders, both of whom have full-time office jobs.
- Most customers are coworkers, friends, and relatives. Customers also share the product with others, creating referrals.
- At least one customer outside the founders' immediate network found the WhatsApp number on the packaging and ordered through a referral.
- Repeat demand exists: some customers reorder whenever a preorder opens, and some ask when ordering will reopen.
- A typical preorder cycle produces approximately **50 items from 10–20 buyers** across the two offices.
- At the founder's office, a typical cycle is around 7–10 buyers and 15–20 items; a peak observed cycle was around 15 buyers and 30–40 items.
- Orders at the other founder's office can be more concentrated, with a single buyer sometimes ordering up to 10 items.

### 2.2 Current operational problem

**LOCKED:** Demand is constrained by inconsistent ordering availability rather than clear evidence of insufficient demand.

The current preorder process depends on the founders manually opening a WhatsApp list for one or two days, copying the orders to a private founder group, buying supply, arranging packing, distributing orders, and reconciling payments manually. The opening cadence has varied from weekly to fortnightly to only when the founders have time.

The target operating model is:

> Discover → order at any time the store is accepting orders → reserve materials → aggregate demand → replenish if needed → pack in batches → fulfill on a promised date → collect and reconcile payment → reorder

"Always on" means customers can place future orders whenever the store is open for fulfillment. It does **not** mean Le Nouette promises fulfillment every day of the year.

### 2.3 Operating principles

**LOCKED**

1. Customer ordering should require roughly the same effort as sending a short WhatsApp message.
2. The storefront should remain simple even if the system behind it is sophisticated.
3. Founders should see the same source of truth for orders, fulfillment, stock, payments, receivables, and business health.
4. Finished products are made predominantly to order; the main buffer is held as supplier/raw stock and packaging.
5. Packing is performed in aggregate by product quantity, not customer by customer.
6. The helper does not access Founder OS or customer/financial data.
7. Office fulfillment has zero incremental transport cost because products travel during the founders' normal commute.
8. External last-mile delivery is paid by the customer and treated as a pass-through, not product COGS.
9. When Le Nouette changes availability after accepting an order, the owners absorb the inconvenience rather than pushing it onto the customer.

## 3. Brand positioning and identity

### 3.1 Positioning

**LOCKED**

- Brand qualities: **premium, family-made, indulgent**.
- Positioning: **accessible premium**, not luxury gourmet.
- Consumption occasions are everyday and social: office desks, meetings, family gatherings, friends, and sharing.
- French influence should be subtle; the name carries the French character without relying on stereotypical pâtisserie or luxury cues.
- Avoid excessive gold, ornate crests, black-and-gold luxury styling, or an identity that resembles perfume or gift-shop branding.

### 3.2 Brand meaning

**LOCKED:** "Nouette" was selected from a French word associated with a ribbon or small tie. The brand idea is a small tie that brings people together. The snack should act as a medium for sharing stories and conversation.

### 3.3 Identity architecture

**LOCKED**

- Preferred symbol direction: the **Nouette Knot**, a continuous ribbon/knot representing connection.
- Preferred character: warm, connected, timeless, clean, and elegant without becoming overly traditional.
- The symbol should be structurally clean with soft, flowing curves and remain legible at small sticker sizes.
- The symbol should remain abstract rather than depict a cheese stick, allowing expansion into other food categories.
- **Nouette Knot** = symbol of connection.
- **LE NOUETTE** = master brand.
- **Gourmet Cheese Sticks** = product/category descriptor, never part of the master logo.
- The round lid treatment is symbol-led; the rectangular product label gives the name and product information greater prominence.
- The mark must work in flat, single-color printing. Foil or premium finishes may enhance it but must not be required.
- The current preferred direction is a refined Classic Knot concept; final production artwork is not yet locked.

### 3.4 Color and seasonal system

**LOCKED**

- Everyday anchor palette: deep burgundy or warm maroon with warm cream.
- The identity may use a restrained cheese-gold accent.
- Seasonal variants may introduce different premium palettes while preserving the symbol, wordmark, typography, and ribbon language.
- Candidate seasonal directions include deep green or muted sage for Lebaran, and forest green, cranberry, or other warm festive tones for Christmas.

### 3.5 Packaging identity

**LOCKED**

- Milieu uses a clear plastic jar from top to bottom, including a plastic lid.
- The jar lid receives one round brand sticker.
- The jar body receives one portrait-oriented square/rectangular product sticker.
- Grande uses one square product sticker on its plastic pouch.
- There is no dedicated back sticker in the current packaging cost structure.
- Round sticker: master brand only—Nouette Knot and LE NOUETTE.
- Square/portrait sticker: product/category, weight, required information, and future ordering QR.
- The label should not add unverified claims such as "premium cheese" or "no artificial preservatives."
- The ordering QR should link directly to the Le Nouette storefront and turn packaging into a repeat-order/referral channel.
